01 Shift Configuration

Adjust shift lengths for this period, or turn off Shift 4 entirely for stretches of the year when only 3 shifts are needed. This only applies to the period selected above. Shift 1's start still follows sunrise automatically — only its end time is editable here.

02 Shift Timeline

03 Set Weekly Availability

Add a name, pick one shift, and the days of the week they can cover it — e.g. "2nd shift, Mon/Wed/Fri only." This only applies to the period selected in the tabs above — it won't carry over to any other 2-week period.

Pick broadly here — use "Select all" on both shifts and days if you're generally open, then use Mark Unavailable further down for the specific times you can't work. That's usually a shorter list than what you can work.

04 Roster Availability Sheet

Everyone who's submitted availability for the selected period, and whether they've filled it out completely. Removing a person requires admin unlock (top right).

05 Mark Unavailable

One-off override for specific dates — e.g. normally available all day Monday, but something's come up and they can't cover Shift 2 or Shift 4 on a few particular days. Pick as many dates as you need. Only dates within the selected period apply.

This only affects the dates selected — their weekly template stays the same for every other week.

06 Unavailable

Everyone's one-off unavailability currently in effect, soonest first. Removing an entry requires admin unlock (top right).

08 Roster —

09 Generate Schedule

Builds one weekly pattern from everyone's templates and repeats it across the whole period — so whatever gets assigned to a Monday is the same on every Monday in this stretch. Marked-unavailable dates are always honored, no exceptions. Balances the workload instead of first-come-first-served, never double-books someone on two shifts the same day, and never lets someone open the day after they closed. The 1→2→3→4 rotation is followed whenever possible — it's only broken as a last resort when there's truly no other way to fill a shift, and every time that happens it's flagged below.
