01 Shift Timeline

Drag the edge of a shift block to make it longer or shorter, snapping to the nearest half hour. Shift 1's start follows sunrise by default — drag its left edge to set a fixed start time instead. Changes save automatically.

Reset Shift 1 to follow sunrise automatically

02 Team Roster

Everyone available for scheduling this period. No need to specify shifts or days — everyone's assumed open any time, any shift, unless marked unavailable for something specific below.

Pick a team member, then click a shift on any date to mark them unavailable for it — click again to make them available again.

03 Generate Schedule

Builds one weekly pattern from everyone on the roster and repeats it across the whole period — so whatever gets assigned to a Monday is the same on every Monday in this stretch. Marked-unavailable dates are always honored, no exceptions. Balances the workload as evenly as possible across everyone instead of first-come-first-served, and never double-books someone on two shifts the same day. The only other rule: nobody opens the shift the day right after they closed the last one.
